About CareForce

Supporting better healthcare environments

CareForce Facility Solutions Private Limited is focused on providing dependable manpower and facility support for hospitals and healthcare organisations. We understand that a clean, orderly and supportive environment contributes to a better experience for patients, visitors and healthcare teams.

Our approach centres on role clarity, staff orientation, professional conduct and responsive coordination with the client’s designated team.

  • Service-focused manpower support
  • Uniform and identity-based staff presentation
  • Site coordination and supervision approach
  • Client-oriented service planning

What We Do

Our Services

Support services planned around each facility’s requirements, scope of work and operating procedures.

🧹

Hospital Housekeeping

Housekeeping manpower for routine cleaning, upkeep of assigned areas, waste-segregation support as per hospital protocols and maintaining orderly surroundings.

＋

GDA Services

General Duty Assistant support for approved non-clinical duties, patient movement assistance and day-to-day support under hospital staff direction.

♡

Patient Support

Assistance with non-clinical comfort and mobility-related tasks within the assigned role and the facility’s policies.

🏢

Facility Support

Manpower support for designated facility operations and common-area upkeep, based on an agreed scope.

👔

Uniform & Identification

Professional staff presentation through role-based uniform identification and employee ID processes.

✓

Supervision & Coordination

Coordination with the client’s authorised representatives to review attendance, assigned duties and service feedback.

Important role boundary: GDA and support personnel perform only assigned non-clinical duties. They do not independently diagnose, prescribe, administer medication, perform nursing procedures or make clinical decisions.
